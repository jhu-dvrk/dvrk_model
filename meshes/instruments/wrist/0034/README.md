The 5 mm wrist and Round Tip Scissors meshes and URDF are derived from
[zixingjiang/dvrk_description](https://github.com/zixingjiang/dvrk_description/tree/2a29763608fd619082dec112f07014256cc62c22)
(commit `2a29763608fd619082dec112f07014256cc62c22`, MIT; see LICENSE).
Sources: `meshes/classic/tool/wrist_5mm/`, `gripper_rts_5mm.stl`, and
`urdf/include/classic/tool.macro.xacro`.

The files have been modified for dvrk_model: 3 mm wrist bores, pivot markers,
shaft alignment and colors, and URDF frames and mimic joints. Mesh units are meters.
